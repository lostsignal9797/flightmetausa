Accessibility

FlightMetaUSA.com aims to provide a website usable by as many people as possible, including people who use assistive technologies.

Our approach

We intend to use semantic HTML, keyboard navigation, readable contrast, descriptive labels and accessible form controls.

Feedback

If you encounter an accessibility barrier, please contact us with the page URL and a description of the issue.
